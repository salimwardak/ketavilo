The Great Gatsby

F. Scott Fitzgerald

فارسی · پښتو · English

Nick Carraway meets the wealthy, enigmatic Gatsby, who hopes to revive his earlier romance with Daisy. Glittering parties sit beside class inequality and privileged irresponsibility. An idealized past and a seductive image of success lead the characters toward damaging choices.

Key ideas

  • New wealth can create spectacle without guaranteeing acceptance by an established elite.
  • Nick participates in events, so his judgments are part of the story rather than a neutral record.
  • Gatsby neglects the distance between a real person and his preferred image of the past.

Something to consider

How does loving a person differ from loving an idealized image of that person?
