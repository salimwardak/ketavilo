A Christmas Carol

Charles Dickens

فارسی · پښتو · English

On Christmas Eve, the isolated and miserly Scrooge encounters visions of his past, present and possible future. Seeing how his conduct affects others prompts a change. Dickens connects poverty and social responsibility with the possibility of personal transformation.

Key ideas

  • Memories and lost relationships reveal a character shaped over time.
  • Compassion matters when it becomes concrete conduct and assistance.
  • Scrooge’s change is more than regret: his treatment of other people changes.

Something to consider

If others described the effects of your conduct today, what would you hope to improve?
