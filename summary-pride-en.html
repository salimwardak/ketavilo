Pride and Prejudice

Jane Austen

فارسی · پښتو · English

Elizabeth Bennet and Mr. Darcy move through a society where wealth and marriage shape social prospects. Pride, rumor and quick judgment distort their early encounters. As new information emerges, each must reconsider both the other person and a flattering view of the self.

Key ideas

  • Austen’s comedy exposes class pressure and the narrow choices available to women.
  • Understanding grows when characters acknowledge errors in their own judgment.
  • The novel distinguishes charm and status from responsible conduct.

Something to consider

When has new information changed a convincing first impression you held?
