Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland

Lewis Carroll

فارسی · پښتو · English

Following a rabbit, Alice enters a world of shifting sizes, meanings and rules. Encounters with strange characters test her sense of identity and logic. Wordplay and reversals of adult conventions make the story playful for children and suggestive for older readers.

Key ideas

  • Apparently natural rules can turn out to be unstable conventions.
  • Alice’s questions challenge assertions that sound authoritative but make little sense.
  • Much of the humor comes from a gap between logical-sounding language and actual meaning.

Something to consider

Which everyday custom would seem strange if you encountered it for the first time?
