The Prince

Niccolò Machiavelli

فارسی · پښتو · English

Machiavelli examines acquiring and maintaining political power in the context of Italian city-states. He separates moral ideals from rulers’ observed behavior, considering force, reputation, fortune and judgment. This historically situated analysis should not be read as making every description an ethical or universal recommendation.

Key ideas

  • Political power depends on institutions, support and capacity to act, not intention alone.
  • A ruler’s public reputation and actual conduct may differ.
  • Circumstances and fortune limit personal skill, making historical context essential.

Something to consider

When effectiveness and ethics conflict, which costs tend to disappear from view?

An original short interpretation; not a replacement for the complete book or professional advice.
