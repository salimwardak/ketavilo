Moby-Dick

Herman Melville

فارسی · پښتو · English

Ishmael narrates the voyage of the Pequod, which Captain Ahab turns into a pursuit of the white whale. Ahab’s private hunger for revenge endangers the whole crew. Alongside adventure, the novel reflects on maritime labor, nature, authority and the limits of human interpretation.

Key ideas

  • A leader’s fixation can become a collective mission that silences competing concerns.
  • The whale carries different meanings; no single explanation exhausts it.
  • Detailed accounts of whaling anchor the symbolism in difficult material work.

Something to consider

When does commitment to a goal become a destructive pursuit?
