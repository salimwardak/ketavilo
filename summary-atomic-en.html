Atomic Habits

James Clear

فارسی · پښتو · English

Clear argues that lasting change grows from small, repeated actions supported by workable systems. Rather than relying on enthusiasm alone, a person can make helpful behavior easier to begin and repeat. Cues, friction, rewards and identity all shape that process.

Key ideas

  • Give a desired behavior a clear cue, such as leaving a book where you sit each evening.
  • Begin with a small action you can repeat, then expand it gradually.
  • Measure repeated practice rather than treating one missed day as failure of the whole effort.

Something to consider

What small change to your surroundings would make ten minutes of reading easier tomorrow?
