The Psychology of Money

Morgan Housel

فارسی · پښتو · English

Housel uses short stories to explain why financial decisions depend on experience, fear, luck and ideas of success as well as calculation. The emphasis is on sustainable behavior and recognizing limits, not on a particular investment or a promise of wealth.

Key ideas

  • People with different experiences may perceive the same financial risk very differently.
  • Consider luck and risk when judging the results of someone else’s decisions.
  • Constant comparison can make a sense of having enough permanently elusive.

Something to consider

When you imagine financial success, are you picturing security, recognition or something else?

An original short interpretation; not a replacement for the complete book or professional advice.
