The Metamorphosis

Franz Kafka

فارسی · پښتو · English

Gregor Samsa wakes to find himself transformed into an insect-like creature. Even then, work and his family’s finances dominate his worries. Changing dependencies and failing communication expose the danger of confusing a person’s worth with the ability to earn and serve.

Key ideas

  • The impossible event is narrated matter-of-factly, creating a distinctive unease.
  • Financial need and family obligation can produce both attachment and pressure.
  • Gregor’s inability to communicate deepens separation from his family.

Something to consider

Do we sometimes measure our own or someone else’s worth too narrowly through usefulness?
