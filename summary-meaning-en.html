Man’s Search for Meaning

Viktor E. Frankl

فارسی · پښتو · English

Frankl connects his account of Nazi concentration camps with his approach to meaning. Responsibility, human connection and personal purpose can matter when enduring hardship. His argument should not be read as praise of suffering or blame of those who suffer; circumstances can severely restrict choice.

Key ideas

  • Meaning may emerge through work, relationships or a response to circumstances that cannot immediately be changed.
  • Distinguish the author’s personal account from claims that can be generalized to everyone.
  • Hope can be tied to a particular responsibility rather than a single grand answer to life.

Something to consider
